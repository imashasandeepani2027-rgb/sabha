Public Services & Application Downloads

The main services provided by the Sabha for the citizens as well as students of the Bingiriya Pradeshiya area can be obtained below. By clicking the button, you can open the form in a new tab and download it as a PDF to your computer or mobile phone.

1. Industry and Planning Section

Building Plan Approval

Application form for getting approval for plans before constructing residential or commercial buildings within the area.

Download Application

Business & Trade Licenses

Application form for starting a new business or industry and renewing trade licenses annually.

Download Application
2. Administrative, Utility & Training Services

Auditorium & Playground Booking

Form for reserving the new auditorium or the playgrounds owned by the Sabha for ceremonies and various requirements.

Download Application

Internship Opportunities

Application form for training opportunities provided in the IT, Administrative, or Technical sections of the Sabha for university and higher education students.

Download Application
3. Health & Environment Section

Environmental License Application

Form for obtaining environmental protection licenses required to run industries or businesses in an eco-friendly manner within the area.

Download Application
4. Finance & Accounts Section

Tender and Auction Applications

Official form for parties participating in various auctions and tender procurement processes conducted by the Sabha.

Download Application
