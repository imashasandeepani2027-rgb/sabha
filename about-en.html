Introduction and Governance Structure of the Sabha

Our Vision

To make the Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha area an exemplary local government authority area in Sri Lanka through a systematic and sustainable development process with the maximum participation of the local community.

Our Mission

To ensure a safer and more comfortable life for the public by fulfilling their needs through prudent guidance and efficient resource management, while upgrading health, road, and common utility services in the area.

Governance Structure

The entire operation of the Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha is guided by the combined contribution of two main bodies:

1. Political Authority
  • Hon. Chairman (Chief Executive Officer of the Sabha)
  • Hon. Vice Chairman
  • The Elected Council of Members
2. Administrative Staff
  • Secretary of the Sabha: The chief administrative officer of the council, managing financial and staff affairs.
  • Chief Clerk: Handles office clerical duties and coordination.
  • Technical Officer: Supervises roads, buildings, and physical development projects in the area.
  • Revenue Inspectors: Manages business licenses, shop rents, and council tax revenues.
  • Public Health Inspector (PHI): Controls health, sanitation, and food safety in the area.

Main Departments

Here are the key departments operating within the council to fulfill public needs efficiently:

Department Responsibility
Administrative Department Staff management, document control, and general coordination.
Accounts Department Budgeting, payments, project allocations, and financial control.
Revenue Department Collection of acreage taxes, business taxes, fair rents, and entertainment taxes.
Technical Department Street lamp maintenance, road carpeting, and building plan approvals.
Health & Environment Department Waste management, dengue eradication, and public market sanitation control.
