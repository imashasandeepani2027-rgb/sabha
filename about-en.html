Introduction and Organizational Structure of the Sabha

Our Vision

To make the Bingiriya area an exemplary Pradeshiya Sabha region in Sri Lanka through a systematic and sustainable development process with the maximum participation of the local community.

Our Mission

To ensure a safer and more comfortable life for the public by meeting their common needs through wise guidance and efficient resource management, while upgrading local health, roads, and public utility services.

Governance Structure

The overall operations of the Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha are guided by the combined contribution of two main authorities:

1. Political Authority
  • Hon. Chairman (Chief Executive Officer of the Sabha)
  • Hon. Vice Chairman
  • Hon. Elected Members of the Council
2. Administrative Staff
  • Secretary of the Sabha: The chief administrative officer of the Sabha who oversees financial and staff management.
  • Chief Clerk: Handles office clerical works and overall coordination.
  • Technical Officer: Supervises roads, buildings, and physical infrastructure development projects in the area.
  • Revenue Inspectors: Manages trade licenses, shop rentals, and local council tax revenues.
  • Public Health Inspector (PHI): Controls public health, sanitation, and food safety within the local area.

Main Departments

Here are the key operational branches functioning within the Sabha to deliver efficient public services:

Department Responsibility
Administration Department Staff control, document management, and general coordination.
Accounts Department Budget preparation, payments, project funding, and financial control.
Revenue Department Collection of acreage taxes, business taxes, weekly fair rentals, and entertainment taxes.
Industrial (Technical) Department Maintenance of street lights, road carpeting, and approval of building plans.
Health & Environment Department Waste management, dengue eradication campaigns, and monitoring public market sanitation.
