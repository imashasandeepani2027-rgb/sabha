Public Services and Application Downloads

The primary services provided by the Sabha for citizens as well as students within the Bingiriya local area, along with the official application forms, can be obtained below. By clicking the button, the form will open in a new tab and can be downloaded as a PDF to your computer or mobile device.

1. Industries & Planning Department

Approval of Building Plans

Application form required for obtaining approval of building plans prior to constructing residential or commercial buildings within the local council area.

Download Application

Business & Trade Licenses

Application form for starting a new business or industry, as well as for the annual renewal of trade licenses.

Download Application
2. Administrative, Utility & Training Services

Reservation of Auditoriums and Playgrounds

Booking form for reserving the new auditorium of the Bingiriya Pradeshiya Sabha or playgrounds owned by the council for public or private events.

Download Application

Internship Opportunities

Application form for university and higher education students seeking industrial or internship training within the IT, Administration, or Accounts branches of the Sabha.

Download Application
